All Night Skate

Date: May 29, 2027
Time: 6:30 PM – Late Night
Location:
54 Rockaway Ave
Brooklyn, NY 11233

We eloped!!!! And now we want to celebrate with all of our loved ones. Join us for food, drinks, and dancing at our favorite Brooklyn bar. All Night Skate. Please wear whatever semi-formal fit makes you feel hot and dangerous on the dance floor.

The night will begin at 7 pm (sharp) and go until late. Come ready to celebrate our love and yourselves for pouring into us along the way, from near and far.

There are two trains close to this location: C Train off the Rockaway Ave stop and the J train off the Chauncey St stop. Trains run 24 hours, with reduced service late at night.

Riis Beach

Date: May 30, 2027
Time: 2:30 PM – Sunset
Location:
157 Rockaway Beach Blvd
Queens, NY 11694

The next day we would love to gather everyone at another favorite spot of ours, Riis beach! Please come for an afternoon of reminiscing, rehydrating, and soaking up the joy of the gayest beach of all time.

Please bring a towel, swim wear (if interested), sunscreen, and plenty of water. We'll have music, snacks and drinks. If it's cold, bring layers! We will have some easy-up tents up to the LEFT (if you're looking at the water) of the public bathrooms with a sign so you can't miss us. There are public restrooms and concessions nearby as well.

For public transit (Q35 drops right at the entrance), map to The People's Beach at Jacob Riis Park. If driving, you can park in the lot for $25 and walk on over.

We will probably stay until a little after sunset!
