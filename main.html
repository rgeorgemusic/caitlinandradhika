Kalyanam (Ceremony)

Date: May 28, 2027
Approximate Time: 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM
Location:
Tera Mera
272 Court St
Brooklyn, NY 11231

We eloped!!!! And now we want to celebrate with all of you. Please join us for our Kalyanam, a Kerala inspired Indian wedding ceremony. There will be appetizers (vegetarian options included), chaya (tea), and queer joy. Indian-inspired outfits encouraged (not sure what this means? text us!) Otherwise semi-formal.

Pending confirmation of auspicious ceremony times, the event will begin at approximately 11am and go until 3pm. Please check back in a few weeks for confirmed event time.

There are many trains close to this location: F/G Train off the Bergen stop (closest) and the 2/3/4/5 and N/R/W trains off the Borough Hall stop.

Riis Beach

Date: May 30, 2027
Time: 2:30 PM – Sunset
Location:
Riis Beach
157 Rockaway Beach Blvd
Queens, NY 11694
+ Add to Calendar
Calendar end time is approximate.

To close it all out we would love to gather everyone at another favorite spot of ours, Riis Beach. Please come for an afternoon of reminiscing, rehydrating, and enjoying the gayest beach of all time.

Please bring a towel, swim wear (if you're into that), sunscreen, and plenty of water. We'll have music, snacks and drinks. If it's cold, bring layers! We will have some easy-up tents up to the LEFT (if you're looking at the water) of the public bathrooms with a sign so you can't miss us. There are concessions nearby as well.

For public transit the Q35 bus drops right at the entrance, but best to map to The People's Beach at Jacob Riis Park and see what route is best for you. If driving, you can park in the lot for $25 and walk on over.

We will probably stay until a little after sunset!
