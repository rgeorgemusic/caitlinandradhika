Date: May 29, 2027

Location:

We wanted the weekend to start with something that feels completely like us—music, skating, and spending the night surrounded by our favorite people.

Riis Beach

Date: May 30, 2027
Time: 2:30 PM – Sunset
Location:
157 Rockaway Beach Blvd

Beach clothes, barefoot vibes, and one final celebration together.
