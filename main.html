Riis Beach

Date: May 30, 2027
Time: 2:30 PM – Sunset
Location:
157 Rockaway Beach Blvd
Queens, NY 11694

The next day we would love to gather everyone at another favorite spot of ours, Riis beach! Please come for an afternoon of reminiscing, rehydrating, and soaking up the joy of the queerest beach of all time!

Please bring a towel, swim suit, sunscreen, and plenty of water and we'll have music, snacks, and drinks. If it's cold, bring layers! We will have some easy-up tents up to the LEFT (if you're looking at the water) of the public bathrooms with a sign so you can't miss us. For public transit, map to to The People's Beach at Jacob Riis Park. You can park in the lot for $25 and walk over. There are public restrooms and concessions available as well.

We will probably stay until a little after sunset!
